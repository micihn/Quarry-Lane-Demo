Questions people ask on the first call

Do I have to switch software?

We work inside your QuickBooks Online file, set up with jobs and cost codes. If you're on a spreadsheet, we move you over in the first month.

What does "free if we're late" mean?

If your books aren't closed by the 10th, you don't pay for that month. No form to fill in. It comes off the invoice.

Can you do my taxes?

We estimate your quarterly payments, file North Carolina sales and use tax, and prepare the business return. Your CPA, or ours, handles the personal one.

I'm a year behind. Is that a problem?

No. Most new clients are six to eighteen months behind. We quote the catch-up once we've seen your bank statements.

All times Eastern. Dana or Luis picks up, with your numbers in front of them if you send them first.
